## The Challenge

ATC Engineering is a well-established contracting firm with a serious portfolio of completed projects. Their previous web presence didn't communicate that at all — it looked like a placeholder, not the website of a company doing work at that scale.

Specifically:
- No project showcase or portfolio system
- Generic template that looked identical to competitors
- No technical credibility signals
- Difficult to maintain and update

## What We Built

### 1. Custom Design System — "Steel & Brass"
A bespoke design system built around the company's brand identity: deep steel grays, brass accents, industrial typography, and grid-based layouts that evoke engineering precision.

### 2. Project Showcase System
A filterable portfolio of completed projects — organized by sector, project type, and scale. Each project has its own detail page with scope, outcome, and project imagery.

### 3. Corporate Presence
Services pages, company history, team section, and a contact system — all building on the design system for visual consistency.

## Technology Stack

- **Framework:** Next.js 14, TypeScript
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS + custom design tokens
- **Animation:** Framer Motion
- **Hosting:** Vercel

## Outcome

A modern digital brand that reflects the company's actual capabilities. Visitors can now understand the firm's project history, evaluate their technical expertise, and make contact through a professional experience.
