## Overview

The **AI Resume Matcher & Gap Analyzer** is an intelligent candidate screening and matching system engineered to eliminate manual resume screening fatigue and reduce recruitment bias.

By ingesting unstructured resume documents (PDF, DOCX) and pairing them with target job descriptions, the system performs entity extraction, vectorized embedding analysis, and similarity scoring.

## Key Capabilities

### 1. Document Ingestion & Text Normalization
Extracts unstructured text and structural formatting from diverse resume layouts using PyPDF and python-docx, parsing experience sections, education, and skill blocks into normalized JSON payloads.

### 2. Semantic Embedding & Similarity Engine
Computes dense vector representations using state-of-the-art NLP transformers and cosine similarity calculations, moving beyond crude keyword matching to capture true contextual relevance and conceptual overlaps.

### 3. Skill Gap Analysis & Recommendations
Identifies missing critical skills, required certifications, and domain competencies demanded by the job description, producing a clear gap breakdown and match score for hiring teams.

## Tech Stack

- **Core Engine:** Python 3.10+
- **NLP & Processing:** Transformers, Sentence-Transformers, SpaCy, scikit-learn
- **Document Parsing:** PyPDF2, pdfminer.six, python-docx
- **API & Interface:** FastAPI, Pydantic
