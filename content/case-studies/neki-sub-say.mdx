## The Challenge

NEIKI Sub Say Foundation is a non-profit working across multiple regions. Before this project, they had no web presence, no online donation capability, and were managing all donor communications manually.

The specific problems:
- No way for new donors to discover the organization online
- Manual donation tracking — prone to errors, time-consuming
- No system to showcase project impact and galleries
- Volunteer coordination happening through WhatsApp

## What We Built

### 1. Public Website & Impact Galleries
A full marketing website with their mission, team, and program areas. The impact gallery system lets the team upload project photos and stories from an admin interface without touching code.

### 2. Donation Platform
Integrated donation flow built on Supabase — donors can give one-time or recurring, and receive automated receipts. The team has a dashboard showing donation totals, donor history, and pending items.

### 3. Admin Panel
A simple CMS-style admin interface for managing gallery content, donor records, and communications — designed for non-technical staff.

## Technology Stack

- **Frontend:** Next.js 14, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS
- **Backend/DB:** Supabase (PostgreSQL + Auth + Storage)
- **Hosting:** Vercel
- **Email:** Supabase Edge Functions + Resend

## Outcome

The platform launched within 6 weeks. The organization went from zero digital infrastructure to a complete online presence — public website, donation platform, and admin tools — enabling them to reach donors they couldn't previously reach.
